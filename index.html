Privacy & Safety
Made for kids — built for parents' peace of mind.
🙅 No sign-up, no account.
No login, no password, no email required. Just open and play.
🔐 No personal information collected.
We never ask for or collect your child's name, email, address, phone number, photos, or location.
📱 Everything stays on your device.
Mission progress and badges are saved only in this browser, on this device. They are never uploaded to a server, and the optional certificate name you type is used only to draw the image on your device — it is not sent anywhere.
Anonymous usage counts only.
We use Plausible, a privacy-first analytics tool, to count aggregate usage (for example, how often a mission is opened). It uses no cookies, collects no personal information, and never tracks your child across other sites or apps.
🚫 No ads. No third-party trackers. No data selling — ever.
👶 COPPA & children's privacy.
This app is designed to align with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will address it promptly.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Play safely.
These missions involve real, active paddle play. Please provide adult supervision and play in a safe, open space clear of obstacles.

🧰 Product Care

Keeping your set working

Does the sticky surface wear out?

Brush the paddle face with a dry hand or soft brush after sandy or grassy play. Lint is what weakens the grip, not use.

Can I wash it?

Spot-clean with a damp cloth and air-dry. Don't machine wash and don't soak — the balls hold water.

Is it pool-safe?

It's a dry-play set. Water on the ball stops it from sticking.

What if the QR card is lost?

Bookmark qr.jumvi.co or add it to your home screen. Your progress stays on this device.
