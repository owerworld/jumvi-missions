## Movement evidence → guardrails

- **Hand attachment:** rear hand and strap remain on the same side of the paddle across a sequence. Fingers may be visible around/above the back, but must not pass through the blue catching plane. Do not mirror one frame so that paddle/free-hand identities silently swap. The exact JUMVI child-hand fit still needs product testing.
- **Release:** show ball leaving the free hand. Do not depict a racket swing, paddle catapult or sticky ball spontaneously launching from its face. A pose is not evidence of a mandated underarm/overarm technique.
- **Approach and contact:** receiving blue face must face the local incoming path; contact is tangential at the front surface with a believable ball/paddle overlap and occlusion. No arrow through paddle/torso, no visible gap labeled contact, no ball embedded inside the paddle, no bounce continuation after sticky catch. Do not require a made-up exact angle.
- **Reset:** show retained ball, then free-hand removal, then new free-hand toss. Static competitor images did not establish a detach grip/force. Use the human-approved sequence without inventing a peel direction or mechanical force.
- **One ball:** distinct frames may repeat the same ball. Within one moment, do not use multiple solid balls as motion ghosts. Ball trajectory and foot route use distinguishable conventions; do not make path illustrations look like new UI controls.

Existing mission step counts are source rules pending physical suitability review, not validated safe spacing. Do not impose one competitive sport stance on every age/body.
